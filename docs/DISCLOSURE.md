# Disclosure

Team Evangelion's design, story, direction and creative decisions are entirely our own. In line with the jam's rules, this file discloses the tools used in production.

- **Code:** Large language models assisted with implementation and debugging, under the team's direction and review.
- **Comic-page art:** Image generation produced the comic pages, working from the team's scene-by-scene art direction, composition notes and iteration. Final selection and edits are the team's.
- **Music and sound:** Some cues, including the lullaby motif and selected effects, were created with sound generation and synthesis. The motif concept, placement and mix decisions are the team's.
- **Writing:** Story text was written by the team, with writing assistants used for iteration.

Everything in this repository was directed, curated, integrated and shipped by Team Evangelion.
